About Me


Hi! My name is Gopi Kurcheti. I’m a Full Stack Java Developer with a
strong passion for building modern and user-friendly web applications.
My journey into development started in 2024 when I was experimenting
with creating small projects and interactive designs.


Instead of just stopping at basic websites, I discovered my real
interest in full stack development — where I could design, code,
and bring ideas to life from start to finish. This opened doors for
me to explore Java, Spring Boot, React.js, SQL, and DevOps.


These days, my main focus is on building scalable and creative
applications, coding things from scratch, and crafting designs
that feel smooth, engaging, and impactful in the browser.
